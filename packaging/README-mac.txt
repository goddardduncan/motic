Moticam Viewer (unofficial) - for the Motic USB microscope camera
=================================================================

Unofficial, free software. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Motic.
Source and licence: https://github.com/goddardduncan/motic

1. Unzip and drag "Moticam Viewer" into your Applications folder.
2. The first time, macOS will refuse to open it because it is not from the
   App Store. Double-click it once, close the warning, then open
   System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down and click
   "Open Anyway" next to "Moticam Viewer". You only need to do this once.
3. Plug the camera into any USB port (before or after opening the app).

Keys (press ; in the window for the full list):
  space    save a photo to Pictures/Motic
  a        auto exposure on/off      s / d   darker / brighter
  p        change colour pattern if colours look wrong
  7        1:1 zoom on the centre, for focusing
  Esc      quit
